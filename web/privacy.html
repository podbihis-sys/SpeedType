Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 2026

1. Introduction

SpeedType ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use the SpeedType mobile application (the "App") on iOS or Android.

This policy is written to comply with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), and the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). By using SpeedType, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Data We Collect

We collect the minimum amount of data needed to operate SpeedType and improve your experience.

2.1 Account Information

When you create an account or sign in, we collect:

  • Display name and avatar (optional)
  • Email address (from Apple Sign-In or Google Sign-In)
  • A unique user identifier generated by Firebase Authentication

If you sign in with Apple, we receive only a private email relay address unless you choose to share your real address. If you sign in with Google, we receive your Google account email and display name.

2.2 Usage Data

We collect data about how you use SpeedType, including:

  • WPM scores, accuracy, and difficulty level chosen
  • Completed daily challenges and streaks
  • Language selection and in-app preferences
  • Session timestamps, crash logs, and performance metrics

2.3 Device Information

We automatically collect certain device information:

  • Device model, operating system, and OS version
  • Unique device identifiers (IDFV on iOS, Android Advertising ID)
  • App version, locale, and timezone
  • Approximate IP-derived location (country level only)

3. How We Use Your Data

We use the data we collect to:

  • Provide and operate the SpeedType service
  • Display your scores on global leaderboards (using your display name only)
  • Sync your progress across devices
  • Analyze usage trends and improve the App
  • Show personalized or non-personalized advertising, depending on your consent
  • Provide customer support and respond to inquiries
  • Detect, prevent, and address fraud, cheating, and abuse
  • Comply with applicable laws and enforce our Terms of Service

Legal basis (GDPR): We process your data on the basis of contract performance (to provide the App), legitimate interests (security, analytics, product improvement), and your consent (for advertising and analytics cookies where required).

4. Third-Party Services

SpeedType integrates with trusted third-party services that may process your data on our behalf or as independent controllers. Each provider has its own privacy policy.

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Analytics, Crashlytics, Cloud Messaging) — firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google AdMob (advertising) — policies.google.com/technologies/ads
  • Apple Sign-In (authentication on iOS) — apple.com/legal/privacy
  • Google Sign-In (authentication) — policies.google.com/privacy
  • RevenueCat (subscription management) — revenuecat.com/privacy
  • Meta Audience Network (advertising mediation) — facebook.com/about/privacy
  • AppLovin (advertising mediation) — applovin.com/privacy

We do not sell your personal information for monetary consideration. Under the CCPA, certain advertising activities may be considered "sharing" or "selling". You can opt out at any time via Settings → Privacy → Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information inside the App.

5. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of access — request a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Right to rectification — correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Right to deletion ("right to be forgotten") — request deletion of your account and data
  • Right to data portability — receive your data in a machine-readable format
  • Right to object / restrict processing — object to specific uses of your data
  • Right to withdraw consent — at any time, without affecting prior processing
  • Right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority

To exercise these rights, open the App and go to Settings → Account → Manage My Data, or email us at privacy@speedtype.app. We will respond within 30 days.

California residents (CCPA/CPRA): You also have the right to know what personal information is collected, the right to delete it, the right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information, and the right to non-discrimination for exercising your rights.

6. Cookies and Similar Technologies

SpeedType is a mobile app and does not use browser cookies directly. However, our third-party SDKs (AdMob, Firebase Analytics, AppLovin) may use mobile identifiers, SDK tokens, and local storage for similar purposes: remembering your preferences, measuring performance, and delivering advertising.

On iOS, advertising tracking requires your explicit consent via the App Tracking Transparency prompt. On Android, you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in system settings. In the EEA, UK, and Switzerland, we show a consent dialog compliant with the IAB Transparency & Consent Framework.

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

SpeedType is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we learn that we have collected personal data from a child without verified parental consent, we will delete that information as soon as possible.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal data, please contact privacy@speedtype.app.

8. Data Retention

We retain your personal data only for as long as is necessary for the purposes set out in this policy:

  • Account data: retained as long as your account is active
  • Scores and leaderboard entries: retained indefinitely unless you delete your account
  • Crash and analytics logs: retained for up to 90 days
  • Advertising identifiers: retained according to the advertising provider's policy (typically 13 months)

When you delete your account, we delete or anonymize your personal data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law or for legitimate business purposes (for example, fraud prevention).

9. International Data Transfers

SpeedType is operated from the European Union, but data may be stored and processed in other countries where our service providers operate, including the United States. When we transfer data outside the EEA, UK, or Switzerland, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) or adequacy decisions.

10. Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures to protect your data, including encryption in transit (TLS), secure authentication, access controls, and regular security audits. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page and, for material changes, notify you inside the App. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

12. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, your data, or your rights, please contact us:

  • Email: privacy@speedtype.app
  • Website: speedtype.app

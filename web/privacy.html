Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 2026

1. Introduction

SpeedType ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use the SpeedType mobile application (the "App") and related services.

This policy is designed to comply with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) for users in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA) for users in California. By using SpeedType, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2. Data We Collect

We collect several types of information to provide and improve the App:

2.1 Account Information

  • Display name and avatar (optional)
  • Email address (if you sign in with Apple or Google)
  • Unique user identifier generated by our authentication provider
  • Language preferences and settings

2.2 Usage Data

  • Typing test results (WPM, accuracy, test duration)
  • Daily challenge participation and streaks
  • Leaderboard positions and personal records
  • In-app interactions, screens viewed, and features used
  • Session timestamps and crash reports

2.3 Device Information

  • Device model, operating system and version
  • Language and region settings
  • Anonymous advertising identifier (IDFA / AAID) — only if you consent
  • IP address (collected temporarily for abuse prevention and geolocation at country level)

3. How We Use Your Data

We process your personal data for the following purposes, with the following legal bases under GDPR:

  • Providing the service (contract): authenticate you, save your progress, power leaderboards and daily challenges.
  • Improving the App (legitimate interest): analyze usage patterns, fix bugs, and ship new features.
  • Personalized advertising (consent): show relevant ads through third-party networks, only if you opt in.
  • Fraud prevention and abuse detection (legitimate interest): detect cheating, bots, and leaderboard manipulation.
  • Communication (legitimate interest): respond to support requests.

4. Third-Party Services

SpeedType integrates with the following trusted third-party providers. Each operates under its own privacy policy, which we encourage you to review.

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Analytics, Crashlytics) — stores account data, game progress and anonymous analytics. Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob — serves advertisements. Privacy Policy
  • Apple Sign-In — optional authentication method. Privacy Policy
  • Google Sign-In — optional authentication method. Privacy Policy
  • RevenueCat — manages in-app subscriptions and entitlements. Privacy Policy
  • Meta Audience Network — serves advertisements via our mediation stack. Privacy Policy
  • AppLovin — serves advertisements via our mediation stack. Privacy Policy

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. Some ad partners may qualify as "sharing" under CCPA when you opt in to personalized ads; you can opt out at any time (see Section 5).

5. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of access — request a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Right to rectification — correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten") — request that we delete your account and associated data.
  • Right to data portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Right to object and restrict processing — for processing based on legitimate interest.
  • Right to withdraw consent — at any time, without affecting prior processing.
  • Right to opt out of sale/sharing (CCPA) — California residents can opt out of personalized advertising.
  • Right to lodge a complaint — with your local data protection authority.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at privacy@speedtype.app. We will respond within 30 days. You can also delete your account directly from the Settings screen in the App.

6. Cookies and Similar Technologies

SpeedType is a mobile application and does not use traditional browser cookies. However, we and our partners use similar technologies such as local storage, device identifiers, and SDKs to:

  • Keep you signed in
  • Remember your preferences
  • Measure performance and analytics
  • Deliver and measure advertising (only with your consent)

On first launch, users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland see a consent dialog where you can accept or reject non-essential tracking. You can update your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy.

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

SpeedType is not directed at children under 13 years of age (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If you are a parent or guardian and you believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at privacy@speedtype.app and we will promptly delete that information from our records.

8. Data Retention

We retain your personal data only as long as necessary to provide the service and fulfill the purposes described in this policy:

  • Account data: retained while your account is active. Deleted within 30 days of account deletion.
  • Game results and leaderboard entries: retained until account deletion; aggregated anonymous statistics may be kept indefinitely.
  • Analytics data: retained for up to 14 months in anonymized form.
  • Crash logs: retained for up to 90 days.

9. International Data Transfers

SpeedType is operated globally, and your data may be processed in countries outside your country of residence, including the United States. Where data is transferred out of the European Economic Area, we rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) and other appropriate safeguards to ensure your data receives equivalent protection.

10. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction. This includes encryption in transit (TLS), encryption at rest, access controls, and regular security reviews. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will notify you through the App or by updating the "Last Updated" date above. Please review this policy periodically.

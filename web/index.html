Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you wanted to know about SpeedType.

What is a good WPM on mobile?

On mobile, most people type between 25 and 40 WPM with two thumbs. Anything above 50 WPM is considered fast, and 70+ WPM puts you in the top 1% of mobile typists. SpeedType helps you benchmark and improve from wherever you start.

Is SpeedType free?

Yes. SpeedType is free to download and play, with unlimited tests, daily challenges, and leaderboard access. We offer an optional ad-free subscription for users who prefer a cleaner experience, but every core feature is available for free.

Which languages are supported?

SpeedType currently supports English, German, Spanish, French, and Portuguese. Each language uses native word lists and typing conventions so your results reflect real-world speed.

How does the Daily Challenge work?

Every day at midnight (your local time), a fresh challenge unlocks with the same text for all players. You get one attempt per day, and your result is added to the global leaderboard. Maintain your streak to earn bonuses and climb the rankings.

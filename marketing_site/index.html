Frequently Asked Questions

What is a good WPM on mobile?
Most mobile typists land between 30 and 45 WPM. Anything above 60 WPM on a phone is excellent. SpeedType's leaderboard shows the current top scores globally in each language and difficulty level.
Is SpeedType free?
Yes. SpeedType is free to download and play on iOS and Android. The core typing tests, daily challenge, and leaderboard are completely free. An optional Pro subscription removes ads and unlocks advanced analytics.
Which languages are supported?
SpeedType currently supports five languages: English, German, Spanish, French, and Portuguese. Each language has five difficulty levels and a separate leaderboard.
How does the Daily Challenge work?
Every day at 00:00 UTC a new challenge text is published. All players worldwide type the same text, and the global leaderboard resets daily. Build a streak by completing the challenge every day.
